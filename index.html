Codebreaker (Working Title)

A game I'm working on during my underemployment. The core concept is a horror game with survival elements where the player is locked in a bunker and forced to intercept radio signals to afford food. Over time the radio signals get creepier and more of the secrets of the bunker reveal themselves, but the player also customizes thier new home to make life a little easier. I'm making this game completely on my own with limited free assets like textures and sound effects and a starter setup for player movement (that I've already completely overhauled). I've got features such as

  • Player needs: Eating, drinking, sleeping
  • Basic interactions (grabbing and throwing most items, using certain items)
  • An interactive computer that gets passed input events when being looked at
  • A radio system where signals pop up that you have to tune to a frequency to hear (also radio stations that loop music)
  • A wiring system for connecting lights to switches and connecting more monitors to the PC
